About Jotio

Jotio is a fast-capture inbox for your thoughts and ideas — built for notes you don't need to keep. Capture in seconds, review what matters, and move important notes into your long-term apps. Everything else is archived automatically.

Plain text only. No formatting, no Markdown, no checklists, no attachments, no complex system. Just a simple place for temporary notes — intentionally kept simple. When something becomes important, move it where it belongs. Everything else fades away.

Jotio is universal across the Apple ecosystem. Capture from the iPhone Lock Screen, from a floating window on the Mac, or straight from your Apple Watch. A built-in review flow makes going through your notes effortless, and on-device AI can turn notes into emails, summaries, or action lists — without ever sending your thoughts to a server.

Key features

  • Fast-capture inbox for fleeting thoughts
  • Plain text only — no Markdown, no formatting, no attachments
  • Colors and favorites instead of folders or tags
  • Built-in review flow plus automatic archiving
  • Lock Screen widgets, app-icon long-press, Shortcuts, Spotlight
  • Floating window and system-wide capture helper on Mac
  • Quick capture from Apple Watch
  • Private on-device AI — notes never leave your device
  • iCloud sync across iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch

Story angles

If you're looking for a frame for your review, here are a few angles that make Jotio different from other note apps:

  1. Working memory, not a second brain. Jotio pushes back against the "capture everything, organize forever" trend. Notes are meant to fade. The act of capturing is what matters, not the archive.
  2. No subscription. One purchase, every device. Notes apps have drifted heavily toward monthly subscriptions; Jotio is a single one-time purchase.
  3. Private on-device AI. Useful AI features — turn a note into an email, a summary, or an action list — without sending a single character to OpenAI, Anthropic, or any other cloud service. Everything runs locally.
  4. Built for the whole Apple ecosystem. A real native experience on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch — not a phone app awkwardly stretched to bigger screens.
