How Jotio Works

Capture

Jot down ideas in seconds with the quick capture field. Pick a color dot to instantly organize your thought—no typing categories, no choosing folders. Just tap, type, and move on. Perfect for capturing fleeting ideas before they disappear.

Organize

Your inbox fills with colorful notes that are easy to scan at a glance. Filter by color to focus on what matters right now—work tasks, personal reminders, or quick ideas. No folders. No tags. No notebooks. Just simple, visual organization that actually works.

Review

Swipe through a delightful stack of cards. Flip through your notes to evaluate ideas, or swipe to decide what to do with each one. Share what needs action. Delete what's done. Keep what still matters. Let the rest fade away naturally.

Declutter

Notes you haven't revisited in 30 days quietly disappear. No guilt. No clutter. Your inbox stays light, your mind stays clear. The secret to mental clarity: thoughts that matter temporarily, not forever.
